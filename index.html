What these mean

Download speed
How fast data comes to you. Drives video quality, page loads, and how many devices can stream at once. Measured in megabits per second (Mbps).
Upload speed
How fast data goes from you to the internet. Matters for video calls, posting, backups, and being the host of a game.
Latency / ping
The delay before data starts moving, in milliseconds (ms). Low ping feels "instant." This is the make-or-break number for online and cloud gaming — a fast download with high ping still feels laggy.
Jitter
How much your ping wobbles. Steady (low jitter) is good; spiky jitter causes stutter in calls and games even when the average looks fine.

How this test works

Your browser downloads (and uploads) test data directly from Cloudflare's public speed endpoints and times it — the same technique speed-test sites use. Latency is measured by timing many tiny requests and taking the median; jitter is how much those times varied. The first moments of each transfer are discarded as "warm-up" so the result reflects steady-state speed. Nothing is sent to us — the whole test runs in your browser, and we store nothing.

Results are an estimate of your connection at this moment, from this device. Wi-Fi varies room to room — for the truest picture, test next to your router and again where you actually sit.
